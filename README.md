## The entry

One emoji. Nothing else.

🌅

A sunrise over the sea — the golden-hour moment at the beach, the postcard
image of a dream vacation. It lands instantly: sun, sea, and the promise of
a faraway shore, with no words needed and nothing to explain.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single emoji to `/output/entry.txt`. The judge reads
the entries two at a time and picks the one that most makes a stranger want
to book the flight.

Run it locally to see the output:

```sh
OUTPUT_PATH=/tmp/entry.txt node main.ts
cat /tmp/entry.txt
```
